## Recommended Slide Sequence

For a typical presentation:

1. **Title Slide** (Layout 0) — Cover with title and subtitle
2. **Divider Slide** (Layout 3) — Agenda/overview section
3. **Content Slides** (Layout 5-8) — Main body content
4. **Data Slides** (Layout 21) — Statistics and KPIs
5. **Process Slides** (Layout 20) — Workflows and timelines
6. **Image Slides** (Layout 11-13) — Visual content
7. **End Slide** (Layout 26) — Branded closer
